# Enterprise Refund Policy

Enterprise customers may request a service credit or refund review within 30 calendar days of the billing event. Requests must include the invoice number, affected workspace and a short explanation. Approved refunds are generally returned to the original payment method. Annual contracts are reviewed by the finance operations team before a refund is issued.

Refund requests related to service outages should include the outage window and a support ticket reference. Credit calculations are based on the impacted service period rather than the full subscription value.
