# FY2026 Annual Report — Management Discussion

Revenue grew overall during FY2026, but Q3 showed a regional divergence. North America remained resilient while Europe experienced pressure from lower expansion revenue and higher customer-support activity in two mid-market product lines.

Management identified Atlas Analytics and NovaDesk as the products with the clearest Q3 European pressure. The company plans to improve onboarding, strengthen account health monitoring, and reduce support response time for these product families.

Key risks include longer enterprise sales cycles, concentration of expansion revenue in a small number of strategic accounts, and increasing support costs in rapidly expanding regions. The company is investing in self-service documentation and customer-success automation to address these risks.
